What you just saw

Two gates, and what each one is for.

Gate 1 refuses the key

Six of these attacks never reach a block. A NEAR function-call key names one contract and a list of methods, and can never attach a deposit. Send anything else and the network rejects the transaction outright — the vault does not run, no fee is charged, and there is no transaction hash to show you.

Gate 2 refuses the request

The rest are calls the key is allowed to make. The vault compares the recipient with an allowlist and the amount with a per-payment cap and a rolling daily cap. Your wording never enters the decision — the contract does not read English — but it is recorded on-chain beside the refusal.
